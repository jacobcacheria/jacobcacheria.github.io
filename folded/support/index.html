Folded! support

Folded! counts how many times you fold your iPhone Duo and puts you on one public leaderboard.

Get in touch

Questions, a bug, or something on the board that shouldn't be there: email the support address in the app's Settings. Reports made from a player's card in the app reach us fastest.

Common questions

Folds aren't counting. Counting only happens while Folded! is open. An iPhone Duo counts a fold each time you close and open it; any other iPhone counts by tapping the counter, and a set number of taps makes one fold. A card belongs to the kind of phone that counted its first fold, so a Duo card won't count taps and a tapping card won't count folds.

I want my account gone. Settings, then Delete account. It removes your profile, photo, links, folds and standings entry, and revokes Folded!'s access to your Apple sign-in.

Someone's link isn't theirs. Links with a check mark were proven by signing into that platform. Any other link is typed by the player. Report a bad one from their card.

Ads. Folded! is free and shows ads on the board. Watching one on purpose can earn a reward, and one of those rewards is a day without ads.
