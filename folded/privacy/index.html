Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Folded! is a public leaderboard of how many times people fold their iPhone Duo, or tap on any other iPhone. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Plain version: your profile and your folds are public on purpose, the app is paid for by ads, and we don't sell your data.

What's public

When you join the board, everyone using Folded! can see your username, profile photo, description, the social links you add, your fold count (and if you tap, your tap count), your rank, your minutes at the top, the cheers you've received, the date you joined, whether you fold or tap, and which iPhone model you count on. Please don't put anything in your profile you want to keep private.

What we collect

Your account. When you sign in with Apple, we receive a private identifier from Apple. We don't ask Apple for your name or email address.

Your profile. The username, photo, description, and social links you choose to add.

Your folds. Each counted fold, when it happened, and the totals and rankings built from them. On an iPhone without a hinge, taps add up to folds. Either way, counting only happens while Folded! is open.

Safety records. Reports you send about other players, the players you block, and moderation decisions about your account.

App integrity. Apple's App Attest and Google Firebase App Check confirm requests come from a genuine copy of the app, so leaderboard counts can be trusted. This includes a device-level integrity check, not your identity.

Rewards from ads. When you choose to watch an ad for a reward, we record that the reward was granted, what it was for, and your account's identifier (or, if you're not signed in, a random identifier made on your phone) so each reward is given once and cheers stay within the daily limit.

Support messages. Anything you send to foldedhelp@yahoo.com.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, photo library (beyond the one photo you pick), browsing history, or health data. The only thing we know about your phone is its model name, which is shown on your card. We don't use your camera except when you choose to take a profile photo.

How we use it

To run the leaderboard, show player cards, keep counts fair, remove content that breaks the community rules, respond to reports and support messages, and keep the app working.

Who we share it with

Your data is stored and processed by Google Firebase, which runs Folded!'s servers. We don't sell your personal information or share it with data brokers. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect players from harm.

Advertising

Folded! is free and shows ads, served by Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, Google receives information about your device: your IP address (used for a rough location, such as your country), the device model and iOS version, an identifier for this app on your device, and which ads were shown and tapped. We don't ask Apple's permission to track you across other companies' apps, so ads aren't personalized unless you've agreed to that where the law asks us to. Apple's SKAdNetwork may tell an advertiser that its ad led to an install, without identifying you.

Some ads are ones you choose to watch for a reward: a cheer, which adds folds to another player's score, or a boost, which makes your own counting worth more for a few minutes. When you finish one, Google tells our server so the reward can be granted, and that message includes your identifier and what the reward was for.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you'll be asked for your consent before any ad is shown, and you can change your answer anytime in Settings. Google's privacy policy explains what Google does with this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

Folded! is for people 13 and older. If you believe someone under 13 has a profile, report it from their card or email foldedhelp@yahoo.com, and we'll remove it.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account anytime from Settings, then Delete account. That removes your profile, photo, links, folds, and standings entry, and revokes Folded!'s access to your Apple sign-in. Reports you've sent about others may be kept as long as needed to keep the community safe.

Your choices

You can edit or remove your photo, description, and links at any time, block players, and turn off haptics and sound. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data; email foldedhelp@yahoo.com and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app when the change matters.

Contact

Questions? Email foldedhelp@yahoo.com.
